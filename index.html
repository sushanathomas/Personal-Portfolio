Who I am

Hi, my name is Sushana Thomas, I started my career as a Security Operations Analyst, serving as a point of contact during emergencies. That experience sharpened my communication, quick decision-making, and precise documentation. I later moved into social service, where I learned to listen closely, understand different perspectives, and solve problems around people's real needs. Through each stage, what has stayed consistent is my care for people, problem-solving, and community. That drive led me to software engineering, where I build accessible, meaningful digital solutions. I'm building an app for lower-income parents and mothers who need newborn essentials, like car seats and clothing. Using React, Node.js, Express, and MongoDB, I'm creating a marketplace where parents can search and filter free or low-cost items by category and location. My goal is to make finding these essentials simpler, more dignified, and easier to access. Today, I'm continuing to grow as a software engineer, and I'm looking to connect with teams and clients building products that make a real difference in people's lives. If that's you, let's connect. .
